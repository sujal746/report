# Before you send: four fixes to your portfolio

These matter more than any cover text. A hiring manager reads the deck, not your email.

**1. Your years of experience contradict each other.**
The profile says 4+ years and "started at 16". The stats block says "starting at age 15". The contact section says 3+ years. Pick one story — I'd use **"four years, started at 16"** everywhere and delete the rest.

**2. Your role list doesn't match your text.**
The Experience Overview names **ComPho, Groovy Republic, Xweave, Exeos**. The role cards show **ComPho, Groovy Republic, Nodepay, Exeos**. Xweave disappears and Nodepay appears from nowhere. A reader notices. Make both lists identical, and keep Xweave — four named projects is stronger than three.

**3. There are no defensible numbers beyond 100K.**
Add one line under the crypto roles like: "Telegram communities moderated: [X] members; average response time: [Y] minutes; scam accounts removed: [Z]/week." Even rough, honest figures make you look like an operator instead of an enthusiast. If you don't have records, estimate from memory and label it approximate.

**4. The contact section is doing too much work.**
Your phone number is public on a page you're sending to strangers. Decide whether that's intentional — most hiring conversations start on Telegram, email or X. At minimum, put the Gamma link in your X bio and pin it, so @kuldeepswain1 leads somewhere.

**Small polish:** "Bhubaneshwar" → "Bhubaneswar" (the deck and this document should match the standard spelling).
