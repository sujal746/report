## How to read this report

- **A — Live posting verified.** I opened the actual listing page on 20 Oct 2026 and read the role, date and pay from it.

UTC+5:30 covers the European afternoon and the Asian evening — say this in every application.

# Section 4 — Screening: scams, fake hiring and low-quality posts

Crypto community roles are a favourite target for impersonation. Use this checklist before you invest time.

**Hard red flags — walk away:**

1. **Any payment to apply, onboarding fee, "gas fee" or "verification deposit".** No legitimate project charges a moderator.
2. **Seed-phrase or private-key requests** — including "connect your wallet to verify" on a hiring form.
3. **A "test task" that is a downloadable file** (.exe, .scr, zipped "mod tools"). This is the standard malware vector.
4. **Recruiter DMs you first from a new account** and asks to move to WhatsApp/Telegram immediately.
5. **A domain that doesn't match the project** (e.g. `fiber-careers.xyz`) or an X account with a recent join date and a copied bio.
6. **Guaranteed token allocations** as the main compensation, with no product, no team names and no funding trail.
7. **Vague scope + urgency** ("start tonight, we'll discuss pay after the launch").

**Verify in this order:**

1. Find the job on the project's **own careers page** or its official ecosystem board — not just an aggregator.
2. Check the X account: creation date, whether the project's official account follows it, and whether the role is pinned or quoted by a team member.
3. Search the hiring manager's name; confirm they actually work there.
4. Check funding: JobStash, CryptoRank or a funding announcement. A real round is a strong legitimacy signal.
5. Ask for scope in writing before any unpaid trial: channels, hours, timezone, escalation path, pay, payment rail (USDC/USD), and invoicing entity.

**Pay benchmarks (2026, from industry hiring guides):**

| Level | Monthly range | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Junior paid moderator | $500–$1,500 | 4–6 h/day, one platform, English |
| Senior / lead moderator | $1,500–$3,000 | 8+ h/day, cross-platform, escalation authority |
| Multilingual senior | $2,000–$4,000 | Two+ languages, regional ownership |
| Agency outsourcing | $2,500–$8,000 | 24/7 coverage, multiple platforms |

Use these when a project says "we'll pay in tokens" — ask for the USD equivalent and the vesting schedule.

# Section 5 — How to apply (and what to send)

## Positioning

You are not "a moderator". You are **coverage + scam defence + onboarding conversion**. Three sentences that work:

> "I cover EU afternoon and Asia evening (UTC+5:30), so your team gets 8 hours of overlap it doesn't currently have. I've moderated X members across Discord and Telegram, and I cut scam-DM incidents by Y% with a verification + slow-mode + escalation setup. I'll run a 7-day paid trial on your two busiest channels and report on response times, ticket volume and scam attempts."

Fill in X and Y with your real numbers. Specificity is the entire game — the CryptoJobsList guidance is the same: context → what you did → result.

## Template 1 — Discord DM to a community lead (short)

> Hey [name] — I'm [your name], a community mod in crypto (currently [project/community], [size] members). I've been in your Discord for [time] and noticed [specific problem: verification flow, unanswered tickets in #support, scam DMs after announcements]. I run moderation with a simple setup: verification gate, escalation tree, and a daily incident log. I cover UTC+5:30 to UTC+14, which is the window your team is thinnest. Open to a 7-day paid trial on [channel]? Happy to send a one-page plan first.

## Template 2 — X DM (very short)

> [Name] — congrats on [launch/testnet]. I moderate crypto communities (Discord/TG) and cover Asia/EU hours. If you're adding mod coverage around [date], I'd like to be considered — 7-day paid trial, clear metrics, no lock-in. Portfolio: [link]
